
Modern inference engines avoid the $O(N^2)$ complexity in computing $Q^\top K$
by caching the submatrix of $K$.
Given
$\vh[l]$ and $K$,
the submatrix $K_{1..N-1}$ is identical to
the output of the previous iteration,
thus the $N-1$ token prefix can be cached.
The same applies to $V$.
\[
 \vh[l] =
 \left[
 \begin{array}{c}
  \vh[l]_{1..N-1} \\
  \vh[l]_{N-1..N}
 \end{array}
 \right].
 \quad
 K= R(\vt)W^Kh[l]
 % =
 % R(\vt)W^K
 % \left[
 % \begin{array}{c}
 %  \vh[l]_{1..N-1} \\
 %  \vh[l]_{N-1..N}
 % \end{array}
 % \right]
 =
 \left[
 \begin{array}{c}
  R(\vt)W^K\vh[l]_{1..N-1} \\
  R(\vt)W^K\vh[l]_{N-1..N}
 \end{array}
 \right]
 =
 \left[
 \begin{array}{c}
  K_{1..N-1} \\
  K_{N-1..N}
 \end{array}
 \right].
\]
