Given a $2d$-dimensional embedding $\vx$,
RoPE rotates $\vx$ to position $t$ as follows.
It maintains the following invariance that makes resetting the positions from $t$ to $s$ possible:
\[
 R_t\vx =
 \left[
 \begin{array}{cc}
  \cos t & -\sin t \\
  \sin t & \cos t
 \end{array}
 \right]
 \left[
 \begin{array}{c}
  \vx_{0:d} \\
  \vx_{d:2d}
 \end{array}
 \right].
 \quad
 \begin{array}{c}
  R_{t+s}  = R_t R_s. \\
  \therefore R_s\vx = R_{s-t}(R_t\vx). \\
 \end{array}
\]
This maintains the following invariance that makes resetting the positions from $t$ to $s$ possible:
\[
 R_tR_s=R_{t+s}, \quad
 \vx = R_tR_{-t}\vx, \quad
 R_s\vx = R_{s-t}(R_t\vx).
\]
